# Cozy Spring Garden Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Floral coloring books are perennial bestsellers, and "spring garden" searches spike heavily in March-May. Combining the proven "bold and easy" format with a cozy spring garden theme hits the sweet spot of seasonal demand and evergreen appeal. Flower/landscape is an underserved sub-niche within bold and easy compared to animals.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 5/5 — Floral coloring books dominate Amazon bestseller lists. "Adult coloring book flowers" has massive search volume. Spring timing amplifies demand significantly.
- **Competition**: 3/5 — Floral is a crowded category overall, but "bold and easy spring garden" specifically has moderate competition. Most floral books are intricate/detailed, leaving room for bold and easy versions.
- **Timing**: 5/5 — Perfect Q1-Q2 timing. Publish by early April to catch spring gardening season through June.
- **Differentiation**: 4/5 — Focus on "cozy garden scenes" (not just isolated flowers) with bold easy style. Include garden vignettes: potting sheds, window boxes, garden benches with flowers.
- **Production Fit**: 4/5 — Flowers and garden scenes translate beautifully to bold line art. Large petals and leaves fill pages well.

## Target Audience
Women 30-65 who enjoy gardening, spring aesthetics, and relaxation coloring. Also appeals to seniors seeking accessible designs.

## Suggested Approach
- **Title direction**: "Cozy Spring Garden: Bold and Easy Coloring Book for Adults" / "In Bloom: A Bold and Easy Spring Garden Coloring Book" / "Garden Bliss: Simple Spring Flower Designs for Relaxation"
- **Page count**: 40-50 pages
- **Style**: Bold thick outlines, large flower shapes, cozy garden vignettes. Kawaii-adjacent but naturalistic.
- **Key themes/scenes**:
  - Cottage garden overflowing with spring flowers
  - Potting shed with tools and seedlings
  - Window box with tulips and daffodils
  - Garden bench surrounded by blooming bushes
  - Watering can with wildflowers
  - Birdhouse in a flowering tree
  - Herb garden with labeled plants
  - Stone pathway through a flower garden
