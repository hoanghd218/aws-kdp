# Zentangle Inspired Patterns Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Zentangle is a meditative drawing method with a dedicated following. Repetitive structured patterns provide stress relief through focused attention. The coloring book format extends Zentangle to people who want to color rather than draw. Positions well for mental health and mindfulness marketing.
