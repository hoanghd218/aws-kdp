# Gardener's Delight Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Gardening is one of the most popular hobbies in America (55%+ of households), and gardeners love relaxation activities. Garden tools, potted plants, greenhouses, and vegetable patches make charming bold_easy coloring subjects. Strong gift potential for the gardener in your life, especially around Mother's Day and spring.
