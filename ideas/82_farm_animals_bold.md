# Farm Animals Big & Bold Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Farm animals are a staple of early childhood education and play. Cows, pigs, chickens, and tractors are among the first things toddlers learn to identify. Extra-thick bold lines make this perfect for the youngest colorers (ages 2-5) using crayons and markers. Strong year-round demand from parents and preschools.

Toddlers and preschoolers ages 2-5, with parents as buyers. Also appeals to preschool teachers and daycare centers for classroom activities.
