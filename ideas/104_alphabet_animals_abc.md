# Alphabet Animals ABC Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
ABC coloring books are a proven bestseller category combining education with coloring. Each letter paired with an animal (A=Alligator, B=Bear, etc.) creates a structured, parent-approved product. Back to school timing in Aug-Sep drives peak sales.

- **Demand**: 4.5/5 — Top educational coloring category, consistently high demand
- **Competition**: 4.0/5 — Competitive but quality always sells
- **Timing**: 4.2/5 — Back to school peak Aug-Sep, plus evergreen preschool demand

## Target Audience
Kids aged 2-5 and their parents/preschool teachers looking for educational coloring that reinforces letter recognition.
