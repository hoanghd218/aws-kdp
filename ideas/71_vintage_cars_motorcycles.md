# Vintage Cars & Motorcycles Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Classic cars and vintage motorcycles have a passionate collector and enthusiast community that skews heavily male — an underserved demographic in coloring books. Decade-specific designs (1950s muscle cars, 1960s British bikes) provide natural variety and nostalgia appeal. Great gift potential for car enthusiasts and Father's Day.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4/5 — Strong niche audience with passionate buyers willing to spend on hobby items
- **Competition**: 4/5 — <150 competing books, most are low-quality or generic clip art
- **Timing**: 4/5 — Evergreen with Father's Day and holiday gift boosts
- **Differentiation**: 4/5 — Realistic detailed style sets apart from cartoon competitors
- **Production Fit**: 4/5 — Detailed line art works well for realistic vehicle illustrations

## Target Audience
Men 30-65 who love classic cars, hot rods, and vintage motorcycles. Also appeals as a gift from partners/kids for birthdays and Father's Day.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 40 (adults)
- **Style**: Realistic detailed line art with accurate vehicle proportions
- **Key themes**: 1950s muscle cars, vintage Harley motorcycles, classic Porsche/Ferrari, hot rod garage scenes, Route 66 road trip, car show displays
