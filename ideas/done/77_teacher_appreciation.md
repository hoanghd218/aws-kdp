# Teacher Appreciation Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Teacher Appreciation Week (first week of May) and end-of-year gifts drive strong seasonal demand. Teachers are a massive profession and coloring books serve double duty as stress relief and a thoughtful gift. Kawaii classroom scenes and school supply characters create a charming, unique product.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4/5 — Huge profession with established gift-giving occasions
- **Competition**: 3/5 — Teacher coloring books exist but most focus on quotes, few kawaii
- **Timing**: 4/5 — Peak May-June, also works for back-to-school and holidays
- **Differentiation**: 4/5 — Kawaii school scenes differentiate from quote-only books
- **Production Fit**: 4/5 — Classroom and school supply scenes translate well to kawaii line art

## Target Audience
Teachers K-12 and people buying gifts for teachers (parents, students, administrators). Primarily women 25-55.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 40 (adults)
- **Style**: Kawaii with charming school-themed illustrations
- **Key themes**: Kawaii apple on stack of books, cozy classroom reading corner, cute school supplies with faces, teacher desk garden, library scene with fairy lights, chalkboard art display

teacher coloring book, teacher appreciation gift, teacher stress relief, end of year teacher gift, school coloring book adults, teacher life coloring, educator gift
