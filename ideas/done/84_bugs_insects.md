## Why This Idea
Bugs and insects fascinate young children, and friendly cartoon-style illustrations make them approachable rather than scary. Large butterflies, ladybugs, caterpillars, and bees provide educational value while being fun to color. Bold_easy style with thick lines is perfect for small hands and provides a spring/summer seasonal boost.
