# Dog Breeds Bold & Easy Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Dog breed-specific coloring books have surprisingly low competition (only 150-400 books per breed). The gift market for dog lovers is enormous. Bold & easy style makes breeds recognizable and accessible for all skill levels.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 5/5 — Dog owners are passionate buyers, breed-specific items sell as gifts
- **Competition**: 2/5 — Only 150-400 coloring books per specific breed, huge gap
- **Timing**: 5/5 — Fully evergreen with holiday gift season boost
- **Differentiation**: 4/5 — Breed-specific focus with bold & easy style is underserved
- **Production Fit**: 5/5 — Bold dog outlines with simple backgrounds generate well

## Target Audience
Dog owners and breed enthusiasts aged 20-60. Major gift market for birthdays, holidays, and dog adoption celebrations.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 40
- **Style**: Bold clean outlines with breed-accurate features, simple cozy backgrounds
- **Key themes**: Golden Retriever at the park, Labrador with ball, Corgi in garden, Poodle portrait, German Shepherd hero pose, Beagle sniffing trail
