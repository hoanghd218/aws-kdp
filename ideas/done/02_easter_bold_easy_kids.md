## Target Audience
Parents and grandparents buying Easter basket stuffers for kids ages 3-8. Impulse gift purchase.

## Suggested Approach
- **Title direction**: "Easter Coloring Book for Kids: Bold and Easy" / "My Big Easter Coloring Book: Bold and Easy Designs for Little Hands" / "Easter Basket Stuffer: Bold and Easy Coloring Book for Kids Ages 3-8"
