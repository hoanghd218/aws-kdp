# Halloween Cute & Spooky Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Halloween coloring books are a massive seasonal category with predictable high demand. Cute rather than scary approach makes it perfect for young children. Publish by August to capture the full October sales window.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4.5/5 — Halloween is one of the biggest seasonal coloring categories
- **Competition**: 4.0/5 — Highly competitive but cute kawaii angle stands out
- **Timing**: 4.2/5 — Publish by August, peak sales September-October
- **Differentiation**: 4.2/5 — Kawaii cute spooky is less common than scary Halloween
- **Production Fit**: 4.0/5 — Pumpkins, ghosts, cats are perfect kawaii subjects

## Target Audience
Kids aged 3-7 who love Halloween but need age-appropriate cute rather than scary coloring pages.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 30-40
- **Style**: Kawaii with cute faces on all spooky elements, round friendly shapes
- **Key themes**: Smiling pumpkin with a bow, friendly ghost saying boo, kawaii witch cat on a broom, cute candy corn with faces, happy bat with big eyes, trick-or-treat bag with candy
