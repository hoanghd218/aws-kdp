# Vintage Travel Destinations Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Travel-themed coloring books tap into wanderlust — a strong emotional driver for purchases. Vintage/retro travel poster style provides a unique aesthetic that differs from typical cottagecore coloring books. Spring/summer is peak travel dreaming season. The bold poster-art style naturally translates to bold & easy coloring pages with large color blocks and iconic landmarks.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4/5 — Travel coloring books have steady demand. "Vintage travel poster" aesthetic is trending in home decor and art. Wanderlust drives emotional purchases.
- **Competition**: 4/5 — Some travel coloring books exist, but "bold and easy vintage travel" is a niche gap. Most travel books are either too detailed or photographic-style.
- **Timing**: 4/5 — Q2 is perfect: spring break planning, summer vacation dreaming, travel season excitement.
- **Differentiation**: 3/5 — Vintage poster art style is distinctive. Bold & easy makes it accessible. Can include destination name in retro lettering.
- **Production Fit**: 4/5 — Vintage poster style = bold shapes, flat areas, iconic silhouettes. Works well as line art. Some complexity with architecture details.

## Target Audience
Adults who love travel (armchair travelers and actual travelers). Vintage/retro art enthusiasts. People planning vacations who want a themed activity. Gift for travelers.

## Suggested Approach
- **Title direction**: "Wanderlust: Bold and Easy Vintage Travel Coloring Book" / "Travel the World: Bold and Easy Coloring Book of Iconic Destinations" / "Retro Journeys: A Bold and Easy Travel Poster Coloring Book"
- **Page count**: 30-35 pages
- **Style**: Vintage travel poster aesthetic adapted to line art. Bold shapes, iconic landmarks as focal points, retro-style destination name lettering at bottom. Art deco influenced borders on some pages.
- **Key themes/scenes**:
  - Paris: Eiffel Tower with cafe scene
  - Tokyo: Cherry blossoms and temple gate
  - Santorini: Blue dome churches and sea
  - New York: Statue of Liberty skyline
  - Tuscany: Rolling hills with cypress trees
  - London: Big Ben and red phone booth
  - Havana: Vintage car on colorful street
  - Bali: Rice terraces and temple
