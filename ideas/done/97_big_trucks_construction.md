# Big Trucks & Construction Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Construction vehicles are consistently one of the top-selling coloring themes for boys aged 2-6. Excavators, dump trucks, and cranes fascinate young children. Extra-thick bold lines make this accessible even for toddlers.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4.5/5 — Top-tier demand, proven bestseller category for boys
- **Competition**: 4.0/5 — High competition but quality bold-easy books still sell well
- **Timing**: 4.5/5 — Evergreen year-round strong demand
- **Differentiation**: 4.2/5 — Extra-thick lines for youngest age group is underserved
- **Production Fit**: 4.3/5 — Vehicle shapes with thick outlines are ideal for generation

## Target Audience
Kids aged 2-6, especially boys, who are obsessed with construction vehicles and heavy machinery.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 30-40
- **Style**: Extra-thick bold outlines, single vehicle per page, simple dirt/sky backgrounds
- **Key themes**: Excavator digging, dump truck unloading, crane lifting, bulldozer pushing dirt, cement mixer spinning, construction site with multiple vehicles
