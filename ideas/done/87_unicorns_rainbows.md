# Unicorns & Rainbows Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Unicorns remain one of the top-selling kids' coloring book themes on Amazon with massive demand year-round. While competitive, a bold_easy approach with extra-thick lines differentiates from the crowded field of intricate or generic unicorn books. This targets the youngest colorers (3-7) who struggle with thin-line designs.
