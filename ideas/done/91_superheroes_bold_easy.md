# Superheroes Bold & Easy Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Kids love superheroes, and generic (non-licensed) hero designs avoid trademark issues while tapping into massive demand. Bold simple outlines make it perfect for young children who want to color their own heroes.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4.0/5 — Superhero theme is universally popular with kids aged 4-8
- **Competition**: 3.0/5 — Many licensed options but fewer quality generic superhero books
- **Timing**: 4.0/5 — Evergreen demand year-round
- **Differentiation**: 3.5/5 — Bold easy style for younger kids is underserved
- **Production Fit**: 4.0/5 — Simple poses and costumes work well with bold outlines

## Target Audience
Kids aged 4-8, especially boys, who love superhero play but parents want affordable non-licensed coloring books.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 30-40
- **Style**: Bold thick outlines, single hero per page, simple backgrounds
- **Key themes**: Flying hero with cape, hero with mask posing, hero lifting heavy object, hero team lineup, hero saving a cat, hero with shield and stars
