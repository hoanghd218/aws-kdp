# Father's Day Bold & Easy Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Father's Day coloring books are massively underserved compared to Mother's Day. Dad-themed content — fishing, grilling, tools, sports, dad jokes — appeals to both dads who color and kids/partners buying gifts. Bold & easy style makes it accessible for men who may be new to coloring. Publish by mid-May for maximum sales.
