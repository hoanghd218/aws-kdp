# Flowers & Garden Kids Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Flower and garden coloring books for kids combine nature education with creative fun. Simple flower species and garden scenes teach kids about plants while providing relaxing coloring activity. Spring timing maximizes seasonal interest.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 3.5/5 — Steady demand, especially in spring
- **Competition**: 3.5/5 — Most flower books target adults, kids versions less common
- **Timing**: 4.0/5 — Spring peak aligns with gardening season
- **Differentiation**: 3.8/5 — Kid-focused flower book is a less crowded niche
- **Production Fit**: 3.5/5 — Realistic flower outlines require moderate detail

## Target Audience
Kids aged 5-10, especially girls, who enjoy nature, gardening, and learning about different flower types.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 30-40
- **Style**: Realistic but simplified flower outlines, labeled species, garden backgrounds
- **Key themes**: Sunflower in a garden, rose bush with butterflies, tulip field rows, watering can and seedlings, garden with birdhouse, daisy bouquet in a vase
