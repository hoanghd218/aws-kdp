# Nurse Life Appreciation Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Nurse appreciation is a strong gift market, especially around National Nurses Week (May 6-12). Profession-specific coloring books sell well because they feel personal. Combining funny nurse humor with appreciative kawaii scenes creates a product that works as both self-care and a thoughtful gift from patients or coworkers.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4/5 — Large profession (4M+ nurses in US) with strong gift-giving culture
- **Competition**: 3/5 — Some nurse coloring books exist but most are quote-heavy, few kawaii
- **Timing**: 4/5 — Peak sales around Nurses Week in May, decent year-round
- **Differentiation**: 4/5 — Kawaii style with humor stands out from typical swear-word nurse books
- **Production Fit**: 4/5 — Kawaii nurse characters and hospital scenes work well as line art

## Target Audience
Nurses, nursing students, and people buying gifts for nurses. Primarily women 22-50. Strong institutional bulk-buy potential from hospitals and clinics.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 40 (adults)
- **Style**: Kawaii with funny and heartwarming nurse-themed illustrations
- **Key themes**: Kawaii nurse with giant coffee cup, cozy nurse station scene, nurse superhero cape, cute medical supplies characters, night shift survival scene, nurse with grateful patient flowers

nurse coloring book, nurse appreciation gift, nurses week gift, nursing student gift, nurse life coloring, nurse stress relief, healthcare worker coloring book
