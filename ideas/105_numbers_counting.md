# Numbers & Counting Coloring Book

## Why This Idea
Number recognition and counting books are essential for toddler development. Combining numbers 1-20 with countable objects creates an interactive educational experience. The toddler market is massive and parents actively seek these products.

## Market Analysis
- **Demand**: 4.5/5 — Core educational category for toddlers, very high demand
- **Competition**: 4.0/5 — Competitive but quality bold-easy entries still perform
- **Timing**: 4.0/5 — Back to school peak plus year-round preschool demand
- **Differentiation**: 4.0/5 — Bold easy for youngest kids, counting objects adds interactivity
- **Production Fit**: 4.0/5 — Numbers with simple objects are perfect for bold outlines

## Target Audience
Kids aged 2-5 and their parents/caregivers looking for educational coloring that teaches number recognition and counting skills.

## Suggested Approach
- **Page count**: 30-40
- **Style**: Extra-bold thick outlines, large number with matching count of objects, simple layouts
- **Key themes**: Number 1 with one star, number 3 with three apples, number 5 with five butterflies, number 10 with ten balloons, counting animals in a row, number tracing with coloring
