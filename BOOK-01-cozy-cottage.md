## 4. Line-art style guide (THE bold & easy promise)

Every page must read as **bold & easy**: thick, uniform outlines · big simple shapes · NO shading, NO fill, NO tiny detail · friendly rounded forms · one clear focal scene · generous white space. This is what the niche buys (large, low-eye-strain) AND what AI draws most reliably.

**Master generation prompt (per scene — fill `{SCENE}`):**
```
Black and white coloring book page for adults, line art only.
Subject: {SCENE}, in a warm cozy cottagecore style.
Style: BOLD and EASY — thick uniform black outlines (heavy line weight),
large simple shapes, minimal interior detail, rounded friendly forms,
clear single focal composition, plenty of open space to color.
NO shading, NO grayscale, NO color, NO cross-hatching, NO text, NO watermark.
Pure white background. Clean closed outlines suitable for coloring.
Vertical 8.5x11 portrait, centered with margin.
```
- Engine: `/image-gen` (Nano Banana Pro) or `/design-grid` for batch concept sheets, then refine winners solo.
- **Output spec for KDP:** each final page **300 DPI**, ~2550×3300 px (8.5×11 minus margins), pure 1-bit/black-on-white. Convert/clean to crisp black lines (no gray AA halos) before assembling the PDF.
- Keep line weight + framing **consistent across all 50** so the book feels like one set.
