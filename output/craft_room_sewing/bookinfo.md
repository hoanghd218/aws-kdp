## Description

<h4>Escape into the coziest craft room you've ever visited — thread your needle, pick up your favorite coloring tool, and unwind one stitch at a time.</h4>
<br>
Cozy Craft Room is a charming adult coloring book filled with 40 kawaii-style illustrations of adorable animals enjoying their favorite fiber arts and crafting hobbies. From a little cat at a vintage sewing machine to a bunny embroidering flowers in an armchair, every page invites you to slow down, breathe deep, and lose yourself in the simple joy of color and craft.

Each illustration is drawn with thick, clean outlines and large open shapes — bold and easy enough to color in a single relaxing session. Whether you reach for colored pencils, gel pens, or fine-tip markers, these pages are designed to feel satisfying and stress-free from the very first stroke.
