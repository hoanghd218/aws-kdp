## Description

<h4>Step into a warm, flour-dusted kitchen where adorable animals bake pies, frost cupcakes, and knead fresh bread in the coziest scenes you can color.</h4>
<br>
Curl up with your favorite coloring supplies and escape into 50 charming baking scenes filled with kawaii animal characters in vintage kitchens. From a bear kneading sourdough to a bunny decorating a three-tier cake, every page invites you into a world of warmth, comfort, and delicious creativity. Each illustration is drawn with love in a cute cottagecore style — detailed enough to be satisfying, simple enough to be relaxing.
