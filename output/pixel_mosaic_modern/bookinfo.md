## Description

<h4>What if every coloring page was a mini masterpiece waiting to be unlocked — one square at a time?</h4>
<br>
Pixel Mosaic is a bold and satisfying coloring experience inspired by retro pixel art and modern mosaic design. Each page features a stunning subject — from a majestic lion face to a cozy reading nook, a Japanese torii gate to an underwater coral reef — all reimagined as beautiful grid-based pixel art.
