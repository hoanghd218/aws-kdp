## Description

<h4>Step into the coziest living rooms and feel the warmth of home on every page.</h4>
<br>
This delightful coloring book is a celebration of cozy interior spaces and the cottagecore lifestyle. Each page features a beautifully illustrated living room filled with plush sofas draped in quilts, crackling fireplaces, overflowing bookshelves, steaming cups of tea on side tables, soft candlelight, and houseplants in every corner. Every scene is drawn with bold, thick outlines and a warm, hygge-inspired aesthetic that makes coloring feel like wrapping yourself in a cozy blanket.
