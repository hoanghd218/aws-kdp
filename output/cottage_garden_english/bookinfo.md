## Description

<h4>Wander through blooming English cottage gardens overflowing with roses, lavender, and old-world charm.</h4>
<br>
This coloring book brings the timeless beauty of English cottage gardens to your fingertips. Each page features lovingly illustrated scenes of climbing roses on stone walls, winding garden paths, quaint potting sheds, birdbaths among wildflowers, and charming thatched-roof cottages surrounded by lush borders. Every design is drawn with bold, easy-to-color outlines and a warm, cozy aesthetic.
