## Description

<h4>Step inside the coziest cafe in the world — where kawaii animal baristas craft perfect lattes and every corner invites you to slow down and color.</h4>
<br>
Coffee Shop Vibes is a delightful adult coloring book filled with 40 charming cafe scenes that capture the warm, relaxing magic of your favorite coffee shop. From a sweet cat barista pouring latte art to a sleepy fox curled by the fireplace, every page is a cozy escape drawn with love in a cute cottagecore aesthetic — bold outlines, open shapes, and just the right amount of detail.
<br><br>
Whether you're a devoted coffee lover or simply crave a calming creative outlet, these illustrations will transport you to a world of steaming mugs, rustic wooden counters, rainy window seats, and pastry-filled display cases. It's the perfect way to unwind after a long day.
