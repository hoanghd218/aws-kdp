## Description

<h4>Discover the delicate beauty of pressed flowers and vintage botanical art in every page.</h4>
<br>
Inspired by the timeless art of flower pressing and antique herbarium collections, this coloring book features graceful arrangements of dried flowers, fern fronds, and botanical specimens. Each illustration captures the elegant simplicity of vintage pressed flower art with bold, easy-to-color outlines. From single stems to elaborate floral compositions, every page is a gentle invitation to slow down and create something beautiful.
