## Description

<h4>Curl up with adorable cats living their coziest lives in every charming scene.</h4>

This delightful coloring book celebrates the everyday magic of cats in their most heartwarming moments. Watch kitties nap on sun-drenched windowsills, explore cozy kitchens, curl up with books by the fireplace, play in blooming gardens, and snuggle into warm blankets on rainy days.
