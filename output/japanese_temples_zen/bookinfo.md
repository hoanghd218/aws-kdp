## Description

<h4>Escape to the peaceful stillness of ancient Japan — one brushstroke at a time.</h4>
<br>
Step inside serene temple courtyards, wander bamboo forest paths, and linger beside koi ponds mirroring the sky. <i>Zen Garden</i> is a coloring book for adults who crave calm, beauty, and a mindful moment away from the rush of modern life. Each page is a hand-crafted scene drawn in a cute cozy kawaii style — bold clean outlines, wide open shapes, and layered details that invite you to slow down and color.
<br><br>
From moss-covered stone lanterns to wisteria tunnels draped over temple walkways, from raked sand gardens to golden pavilions reflected in still water — every page is a quiet celebration of Japanese beauty and wabi-sabi simplicity.
<br><br>

<br>
&#x2022; Large 8.5 x 8.5 inch square pages for comfortable, relaxed coloring

&#x2022; Works beautifully with colored pencils, fine-tip markers, and gel pens
<br><br>
<b>Perfect For:</b>
