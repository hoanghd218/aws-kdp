## Description

<h4>When it rains, the coziest moments begin — pick up your colored pencils and step inside.</h4>
<br>
Rainy Day Cozy is a charming adult coloring book filled with 35 kawaii scenes of cozy creatures enjoying the simple pleasures of a rainy day. From a cat curled on a windowsill watching raindrops, to a bear reading in an armchair by the fire, each page captures that warm, hygge feeling of being safe and snug while the rain falls outside.
<br><br>
Whether you love the patter of rain on the roof or the smell of fresh tea on a stormy afternoon, these illustrations will transport you to the coziest corners imaginable — a bookshop café, a blanket fort, a glass conservatory, a pottery studio, and so much more.
<br><br>
