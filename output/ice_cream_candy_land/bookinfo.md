## Description

<h4>Welcome to the sweetest coloring adventure your little one will ever have!</h4>
<br>
This yummy coloring book is a candy-colored dream come true for kids who love sweet treats. Each page features a big, bold, and easy-to-color illustration of everyone's favorite desserts — towering ice cream sundaes, swirly lollipops, frosted cupcakes, sprinkle-covered donuts, gummy bears, cotton candy, and so much more. Every design is simple, centered, and perfectly sized for little hands to color with crayons, markers, or colored pencils.
<br><br>
<b>What's Inside:</b>
<br>
&#x2022; 50 adorable sweet treat illustrations with thick, bold outlines

&#x2022; Simple, big designs that fill the page — perfect for small hands

&#x2022; Single-sided pages to prevent bleed-through
<br>

<br>
&#x2022; Great for crayons, colored pencils, and markers
<br><br>
<b>Perfect For:</b>
<br>
&#x2022; Kids ages 4-8 who love candy and desserts
<br>
&#x2022; A sweet birthday, holiday, or Easter basket gift
