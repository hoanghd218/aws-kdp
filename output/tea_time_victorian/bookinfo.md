## Description

<h4>Step into a world of warm teacups, cozy parlors, and charming Victorian afternoons.</h4>
<br>
Imagine kawaii animals in lace collars and frilly dresses pouring Earl Grey in sun-dappled conservatories, decorating Victoria sponges, tending cottage herb gardens, and hosting the most delightful tea parties you've ever seen. <i>Victorian Tea Time</i> is a love letter to slow living, fine china, and the gentle art of afternoon tea — all brought to life in 50 gorgeous coloring pages.
<br><br>
<b>What's Inside:</b>
<br>
&#x2022; 50 unique cozy illustrations with bold, easy-to-color outlines
<br>
&#x2022; Charming scenes featuring kawaii bunnies, cats, hedgehogs, foxes, bears, deer, owls and more
<br>
&#x2022; Settings include Victorian parlors, conservatories, cottage kitchens, walled gardens, tea shops, and seaside outings
<br>
&#x2022; Activities like baking scones, arranging roses, piping royal icing, embroidering tea cozies, and making lemon curd
<br>
&#x2022; Single-sided pages to prevent bleed-through — color with confidence
<br>
&#x2022; Large 8.5 x 8.5 inch square pages with generous open shapes — perfect for colored pencils, markers, and gel pens
<br>
&#x2022; Medium detail level — relaxing to color, not overwhelming
<br><br>
<b>Perfect For:</b>
<br>
&#x2022; Stress relief and mindfulness after a long day
<br>
&#x2022; A beautiful gift for tea lovers, Anglophiles, and cottagecore fans
<br>
&#x2022; Mother's Day, birthdays, Christmas, or any occasion that deserves something thoughtful
<br>
&#x2022; Beginners and experienced colorists alike — all skill levels welcome
<br><br>
<b>Brew a pot, find your coziest chair, and color your way through the most charming tea party in town.</b>
<br>
Each page is drawn with care to bring you a moment of calm and a touch of Victorian whimsy. Grab your favorite coloring supplies and enjoy the slow, sweet ritual of afternoon tea — one page at a time.
