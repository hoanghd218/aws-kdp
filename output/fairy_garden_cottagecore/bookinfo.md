## Description

<h4>Step into a tiny, magical world where fairies bake pies, tend gardens, and sip tea under spotted toadstools…</h4>
<br>
Escape the everyday and wander into an enchanting fairy garden full of cozy cottagecore charm. Every page invites you to color a new whimsical scene — a kawaii fairy rowing a walnut-shell boat across a lily pond, knitting beside a toadstool umbrella, stargazing from her mushroom rooftop, or making herbal tea in her garden apothecary. These scenes are drawn with warmth and care, designed to slow you down and spark a smile.
<br><br>
With bold, clean outlines and large open shapes, this book is easy and relaxing to color — no tight clusters or fussy details. Just settle in with your favorite colored pencils or markers and let the cozy magic unfold.
