## Description

<h4>Do you love the cozy, meditative rhythm of yarn crafts? Now you can color your passion!</h4>
<br>
Step into a world of cozy craft rooms, yarn-filled shelves, and adorable kawaii animals stitching away in comfort. <i>Cozy Stitches</i> is a charming adult coloring book celebrating the art of crochet and knitting — from granny squares and amigurumi to Fair Isle sweaters and colorful temperature blankets. Each page invites you to slow down, unwind, and lose yourself in the gentle joy of yarn and color.
<br><br>
<b>What's Inside:</b>
<br>
&#x2022; 40 unique bold and easy kawaii illustrations — all crochet and knitting themed
<br>
&#x2022; Cozy scenes featuring adorable animals knitting, crocheting, spinning yarn, and crafting together
<br>
&#x2022; Subjects include: yarn shops, amigurumi, granny square blankets, knitting circles, craft fairs, spinning wheels, and more
<br>
&#x2022; Single-sided pages to prevent bleed-through — use any medium you love
<br>
&#x2022; Large 8.5 x 8.5 inch square pages with open shapes — comfortable and easy to color
<br>
&#x2022; Bold clean outlines — perfect for colored pencils, markers, gel pens, and watercolor pencils
<br><br>
<b>Perfect For:</b>
<br>
&#x2022; Stress relief, mindfulness, and relaxation after a long day
<br>
&#x2022; A heartfelt gift for knitters, crocheters, and yarn crafters
<br>
&#x2022; Birthday, Christmas, or Mother's Day gift for the crafter in your life
<br>
&#x2022; Beginners and experienced colorists alike — simple, satisfying, and joyful
<br><br>
<b>Pick up your colored pencils and enjoy a little stitch-inspired calm.</b>
<br>
Whether you crochet, knit, or simply adore the cozy craft aesthetic, this book was drawn with love just for you.
