## Description

Hit the open road with 50 fun coloring pages! This Summer Road Trip Adventure Coloring Book is packed with bold, easy-to-color designs perfect for kids ages 4-8. From cute cars and camper vans to camping tents, road signs, gas stations, diners, maps, luggage, and roadside landmarks, every page is a new stop on the journey. Thick outlines and simple shapes make it easy for little hands to color with crayons, markers, or colored pencils. Great for keeping kids entertained on long car rides, rainy days, or quiet time at home. Single-sided pages prevent bleed-through so every masterpiece stays perfect.
