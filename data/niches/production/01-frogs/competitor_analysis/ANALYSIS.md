## The winning COVER formula (this is the big takeaway)
The fast-moving cozy sellers (#2 Froggy Days, #4 Little Comfy, #7 Frog Garden, #8 Frog Moments, #1 Frugbert) **all share one look:**

1. **SQUARE** cover (confirms the 8.5×8.5 decision — every cozy winner is square).
2. **ONE big kawaii/chibi frog**, centered, thick bold black outline, blush cheeks, simple rounded shapes. NOT a busy scene.
3. **Solid flat pastel background** (cream, sage, lavender, peach/teal split) — *no* detailed painterly environment.
4. A couple of simple cozy props only: one toadstool, a monstera leaf, a lily pad, a few stars/clouds.
5. **Big puffy BUBBLE-letter title** top third (cream fill, dark outline).
6. **Bold uppercase subtitle band**: "A COZY & CUTE COLORING BOOK" / "EASY & RELAXING FOR ADULTS & KIDS".
7. **Author name at the bottom**, matching rounded font.

The two that break the formula: #3 Cute Frogs (busy painted jungle bg, 40 rev — older style) and #10 The Little Frog (traditional watercolor + line, Summersdale publisher, premium $12.99).

### ⚠️ Our current cover vs the market
Our `cover_prompt` is a **detailed painterly cottagecore scene** (frog on toadstool + lantern + cottage + dusk + many wildflowers, baked title/subtitle/taglines/author). That's closer to the *older* busy style (#3) than the modern cozy winners. **Recommendation: pivot the front cover to the kawaii single-hero + flat-background formula** — it's what's selling in 2026 and reads far cleaner as a thumbnail.

## SEO / listing patterns
- **Title structure:** short brand phrase + ":" + keyword-loaded subtitle. Recurring power words: *Cozy, Cute, Calm, Bold & Easy, Relaxing, Stress Relief, Coloring Book for Adults (& Kids)*.
- **Dual audience:** 6 of 10 say "**Adults & Kids**" to widen the market. Ours is adults-only.
- **Design count as a selling point:** "**40 Bold & Easy Designs**" — we have **50**, a genuine edge; say it.
- **Price:** budget cluster $5.99 (kids+adults, bold&easy), mid $8.76–9.99, premium $12.99. Our **$8.99** sits mid — fine.
- **Pages:** 30–95; ours 50 single-sided is competitive.
- **A+ Content:** 5/10 have it; we plan it → keep, it's table stakes for the top tier.
- **Description length:** winners run 150–470 words with emoji + bolded benefit bullets. Ours ~140w — slightly short; could add a bullet block + emoji.
