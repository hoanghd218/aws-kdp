## KDP Upload Steps

1. Go to [kdp.amazon.com](https://kdp.amazon.com)
2. Click **Create** > **Paperback**
3. Fill in Title, Subtitle, Author, Description, Keywords from above
4. Select Categories
5. Upload `cozy_cats_daily_life_coloring_book.pdf` as manuscript
6. Set trim size **8.5 x 11**, no bleed, white paper
7. Upload `cozy_cats_daily_life_cover.png` as cover
8. Preview and approve
9. Set pricing and publish
